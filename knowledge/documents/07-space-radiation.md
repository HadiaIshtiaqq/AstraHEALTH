# Space Radiation Exposure and Dosimetry

> ASTRAHEALTH educational note (team-authored summary for a hackathon prototype). Not an official NASA document.

## Sources of space radiation
Beyond the protection of Earth's atmosphere and magnetic field, crew members are exposed to galactic cosmic rays and to particles from solar events. Exposure increases with mission duration and distance from Earth.

## Dosimetry
Personal and area dosimeters record radiation dose over time. Cumulative dose is tracked against career and mission limits defined by the responsible medical authority.

## Interpreting dose records
Dose records describe exposure. They do not, on their own, explain a day-to-day change in heart rate, sleep or mood. Health-monitoring software should present dose as mission context and must not attribute an individual health change to radiation without proper clinical evaluation.

## Shielding and operations
During solar particle events, crew may move to better-shielded areas of the vehicle. Such operational events are useful context when reviewing changes in routine, sleep and stress.
