# Nutrition, Fluid Intake and Body Mass

> ASTRAHEALTH educational note (team-authored summary for a hackathon prototype). Not an official NASA document.

## Why nutrition is monitored
Adequate energy intake supports the effectiveness of exercise countermeasures, immune function and overall health. Reduced appetite or missed meals over several days can lead to weight loss during a mission.

## Fluid intake logs
Fluid intake logs describe what was consumed. Logged intake alone does not establish hydration status, which depends on losses, environment and physiology. Logs are most useful when they are complete; a low daily total may simply mean the log is unfinished.

## Body mass measurement
Body mass is measured in flight using specialized mass-measurement devices because ordinary scales do not work in weightlessness. Trends in body mass, measured under a standard procedure, are a useful indicator for the medical and nutrition team.

## Appetite changes
Appetite can change with workload, stress, sleep, motion sickness and food fatigue from a limited menu. Recording appetite alongside meals helps distinguish a short-term change from a persistent trend.
