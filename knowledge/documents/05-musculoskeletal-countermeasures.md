# Bone, Muscle and Exercise Countermeasures

> ASTRAHEALTH educational note (team-authored summary for a hackathon prototype). Not an official NASA document.

## Bone and muscle in microgravity
Without the loading of gravity, weight-bearing bones such as the hips and spine lose mineral density over long missions, and postural muscles lose mass and strength. Recovery after return can take months and may be incomplete.

## Exercise as a countermeasure
Daily exercise on the International Space Station combines aerobic exercise (treadmill and cycle ergometer) with resistance exercise on a dedicated resistance device. Consistent adherence to the prescribed exercise plan, including resistance sessions, is one of the main countermeasures for bone and muscle loss.

## What exercise logs can and cannot show
Exercise logs show whether the planned countermeasure routine was completed. They cannot measure bone density or muscle strength. Bone density requires dedicated imaging, and strength requires standardized testing. Missing exercise records should be treated as missing, not as zero exercise.

## Back pain and musculoskeletal discomfort
Back pain is commonly reported early in flight, partly because the spine lengthens without gravity. Persistent or worsening musculoskeletal discomfort that limits exercise should be discussed with the medical officer.
