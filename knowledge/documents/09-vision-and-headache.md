# Vision Changes and Headache in Spaceflight

> ASTRAHEALTH educational note (team-authored summary for a hackathon prototype). Not an official NASA document.

## Spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome
Some crew members on long-duration missions develop changes in the eye and optic nerve, described as spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS). Changes in near or distance vision can be one sign. Crew undergo periodic eye examinations in flight.

## Reporting vision changes
Any new change in vision should be reported to the medical officer so it can be assessed with the appropriate onboard equipment. Self-monitoring software should record the report and route it for review rather than interpret it.

## Headache
Headaches are commonly reported in flight and can relate to fluid shifts, carbon dioxide levels, sleep loss or stress. A severe or unusual headache, or one accompanied by other symptoms, needs prompt medical review.
