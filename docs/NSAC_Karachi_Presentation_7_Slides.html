The Challenge & Operational Reality

The Deep Space Health Dilemma

📡 1. The 22-Minute Communication Lag

On a transit to Mars, two-way radio signals take up to 44 minutes round-trip. Telemedicine from Flight Surgeons on Earth is physically impossible during medical anomalies.

🌌 2. NASA's 5 Spaceflight Hazards

Astronauts experience Space Radiation, Isolation & Confinement, Extreme Distance, Altered Gravity (bone/muscle loss), and Hostile Closed Environments simultaneously.

⚠️ 3. The Fatal Flaw of Modern AI

Current commercial AI relies on constant cloud connectivity and hallucinates answers. Giving an astronaut a chatbot that guesses medical diagnoses in zero-g is a mission-fatal risk.

🎯 What the Crew Actually Needs
  • Personalized baselines: Knows what is normal for this specific astronaut, not Earth averages.
  • Deterministic safety: Clinical severity governed by immutable rules, not probabilistic LLMs.
  • Actionable care: Tells them exactly what to do next to recover before irreversible damage occurs.

Our Solution & Technical Innovation

Modular Architecture: Safety Before AI

🛡️ 1. InputSense & DataGuard

Ingests raw telemetry and natural conversational check-ins. Detects negations (e.g. "no chest pain") and flags implausible or stale sensor values before analysis.

🧠 2. Dynamic BaselineIQ

Builds a rolling individual baseline. Crucially excludes the last 3 days from the reference window so an emerging pathology never dilutes its own baseline.

🔬 3. 10 Specialist Agents in Parallel

CardioGuard, NeuroMind, SleepSense, BoneGuard, NutriTrack, and MissionContext analyze domain anomalies without cross-contaminating assumptions.

⚖️ 4. Deterministic Safety & Evidence Gate

The Golden Rule: AI never decides triage. Versioned clinical rules assign severity: NORMAL, MONITOR, REVIEW, URGENT. Real agent disagreements are escalated, never averaged.

🤖 5. Grounded Local LLM Explanations

A local offline model (Qwen 2.5 3B) synthesizes clear, empathetic explanations. Every sentence is cross-checked against finding facts by a guard that strips ungrounded or speculative claims.

Astronaut User Experience & Workflow

The Crew Experience: Detect, Understand, Act

💬 1. Natural Voice / Text Check-in

Astronaut types: "Felt tired and short on sleep, mild headache, no chest pain." System extracts sleep (5.2h), detects negation, flags fatigue without panic, and logs symptoms.

📈 2. Multi-Signal Drift Detection

Detects gradual multi-day changes (elevated resting HR + decreased sleep duration + exercise deficit) that isolated thresholds miss entirely.

🎯 3. Actionable Care Plan

Instead of scary medical jargon, provides 5 targeted recovery steps tied to their personal baseline (e.g. "Target 7.2h sleep for 3 nights; schedule 30m protected wind-down").

🧍 4. Interactive Digital Twin & Routine

Visualizes 2D and 3D physiological stress zones across organ systems and lets the astronaut schedule countermeasures directly into their mission timeline.
